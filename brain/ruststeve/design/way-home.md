# The way home

Issue 19d (`docs/problems/eyes-home.md`). Eyes do not fly in the Nether and no portal can be cast there (water evaporates; obsidian needs a diamond pickaxe), so the bot goes back through its own entry portal: about 475 blocks west of the warped forest through the basalt deltas, on a ledge over the lava sea, its overworld side deep by the overworld's lava sea.

## Key files
- `crates/plan/src/home.rs`: `HomeRules` (`[home]`), `HomeState` (`EnderState::home`), `portal_far`, `portal_cells`, `Plan::home_race_task` (the planner's entry, first in `nether_task`), `home_done`.
- `crates/plan/src/base.rs`: `HOME_STEPS` and `CLIMB_STEPS`, run by the forest base's machinery (`BaseState::steps`).
- `crates/plan/src/pearls.rs`: `pearl_rules` reads `[home] pearls_wanted` in Home; `hunting` stands down once the top-up is over.
- `crates/plan/tests/home.rs`; `Enter`'s done check's test in `home.rs`.
- `crates/plan/src/portal_way.rs` (agent Q6, `docs/problems/eyes-portal.md`): the last leg, `WayRules` (`[portal_way]`), `WayIn` (`HomeState::way_in`), `cut`, `Plan::portal_way`; `crates/plan/tests/portal_way.rs`.
- `crates/plan/src/fortress_way.rs` (agent Q11, `docs/problems/eyes-fortress.md`): the fortress stretch from the blaze spawner's detour to the last leg, `FortressState` (`HomeState::fortress`), `body_in_cell`, `home_way_steers`, `Plan::fortress_way`; `crates/plan/tests/fortress_way.rs`.

## How it works
- **Begun** (`home_begin`) under `Aim::Race`: in the Nether with 12 pearls and eyes held (the `[pearls]` count), from any earlier phase (a resumed bot has none); or in the overworld at stage `pearls_12` or past it (the top-up, the kit and the trip behind it). Note "home: 12 pearls held, on to the way home" or "home: resumed in the overworld".
- **The top-up**: the hunt's rows run on with `pearls_wanted` 14 until 14 are held or `top_up_ticks` 6,000 from Home's start; "home: pearl top-up over" says which. The pearls-done row never runs in Home (it would end the race).
- **The kit** (`HOME_STEPS`, each a base step with its notes): every pearl an eye (Q3's `eyes` row when the table has it, else `home-eyes`, one eye a run, the powder from rods by the needs planner), stems to `home_logs` 16, a table placed with sticks, wooden pickaxes to `home_picks` 2 with 40 uses, sticks to 8, a table to carry. "home: the kit over" (or paused: on without it).
- **The portal**: `portal_far` reads memory's `NetherPortal` index in `portal_chunks` 48 columns round the feet (768 blocks), lowered to its column's bottom portal cell; read again every `portal_every`. With none, `[home] portal` (22, 34, -20: geography, Bridger's ruling; memory holds it at the frontier, probe 45686594347). Note "home: the portal at … (memory|geography)".
- **The recovery** (as `endermen_task`'s): at or under the danger line with nothing to heal with, a sealed pocket ("home: hold in a pocket"); under `travel_health` with a ghast or blaze in sight, cover first. Magma cubes on the trip (the top-up over): one within `CUBE_FIGHT` 3 is fought over the danger line, further ones are left to the legs' shun (8); 2 within `CUBE_NEAR` 6, or any within `CUBE_CLEAR` 8 at or under the line, seal a pocket held until none is within 8 for `CUBE_CLEAR_TICKS` 200 ("home: pocket from magma cubes", counted in `cube_pockets`).
- **The trip**: the heading (`heading_with`) runs legs to `[home] way`'s points then the portal's column, with the travel's rules (`EnderPhase::travels`: shunned piglins and cubes, the fortress turn, the tunnel's spare). The forest at the feet does not end it. `way` is 31 points (none within 24 of the blaze spawner) from the chain's own senses rows (race 45537545167 lane 0, the route east along z 194; race 45350933958 lane 0, the portal to the blaze spawner), reversed, a point every 20 to 32 blocks, ending by the portal; stone and wood pickaxes both count on the trip, and under 100 uses the trip makes stone ones from blackstone or wooden ones from the kit's stems (`TRIP_WOOD_STEPS`, "home: pickaxes on the way") (race 45698618366: straight at the portal the bots stalled at x 448 to 465 in the deltas). With a point, a failed leg turns toward it (`heading::turn_toward`) and a fresh choice is the axis toward it with the most left (`choose_toward`), never away (the exploring choice heads from the seen columns' centroid, and on the way home those are the route back: every bot was sent east). The keep is `HOME_KEEP` 24 in Home; a leg refused only for filler restocks from the walls (netherrack, basalt, blackstone) to the keep and its bridges first ("heading leg N restocks for its bridges", at most `RESTOCK_BRIDGES` 64 over the spare). Turned out, a far walk to within `approach`. "home: on the way" every `note_every` with health, food, pickaxe uses, filler, eyes.
- **The last leg** (`portal_way`, within `[portal_way] reach` 120 on the plane; called just before the approach): the heading stands down (race 45727347488: its legs swung east and west 44 to 64 off, 60 over the portal, turned by crimson signs; the soul sand valley's skeletons shot the other bot 8 times). Standing in the open (air in either cell of the two over the head), a dig straight down `dig_in` 3 where it stands, each floor one the staircase takes ("portal way: dig in at"); then a tunnel (`tunnel::tunnel_toward_covered`: a step with open air in the two cells over the head costs `tunnel::COVER_COST` 8) to the front's tunnel cell (to the portal itself while its chunk is not loaded), which ends within the tunnel's reach 5 of it; then within `cut_range` 12 an exact cut (`cut`: breadth first over level steps and stairs, each the staircase's own refusals, never into the standing cell or the portal) to the tunnel cell itself; at the tunnel cell or the standing cell the approach below takes over (the seals, the walk in). Notes "portal way: the last leg to the front at", "portal way: tunnel to the front at", "portal way: cut to the front at", "portal way: no cut to"; the cut is given up after `cut_tries` 4 with no step nearer ("portal way: cut given up"). Each note counts the pickaxe uses the leg has spent. One dig-in a place (`DIG_IN_APART` 8). The leg budgets 2.5 pickaxe uses a block to go and runs the trip's wooden pickaxes first when short ("portal way: pickaxes for the leg"). The door is boxed before the approach's own seals (`head_seal`: the cells beside the standing cell and its head along the plane and the cell over the head, lava or open, the top first; "portal way: door boxed at": races 45737493003 and 45742933645 lane 1 were set alight there by a fall whose foot the approach sealed); a door filled since (by the lava escape's seal) is dug open again from the tunnel cell ("portal way: the door dug open at"). A covered route also pays `tunnel::HIGH_COST` a step high over its target, so it goes deep first. Under the danger line with nothing to heal with, the leg goes on in rock when not burning and quiet (no hostile but neutrals within 16 for 200 ticks) and its next stretch keeps 2 of rock over the head (`danger_carry_on`), and at the door the approach goes on (`danger_at_door`); else the pocket holds. A danger-line pocket in Home on a ledge (open air beside the feet with a drop under it) digs down one first (`ledge_dig`, at most `ledge_digs` 4: "pocket on a ledge: dig down at"; lane 0 fell off its own pocket, shot).
- **The approach** (within `approach` 24 on the plane): a plain route into a lit portal cell is `Task::Enter` ("home: enter the portal at …"); else a far walk to beside it (bridging lava), else a tunnel to it, each "home: approach N by …". A portal seen out (its cell loaded and not a portal) is lit with flint and steel on the bottom obsidian when held.
- **Lava at the portal**: lava beside or under its lowest cells, and beside or under the open standing cells in front of its two lowest rows, is sealed first with filler from where the bot stands, clicked on a full block beside it ("home: lava sealed at"), at most `SEALS` 24. The entry portal's south face (z -19) has one dry standing cell, (22, 35, -19), with lava at (21, 35, -19), (23, 34, -19) and (24, 35, -19) (probe 45688112814); the north face has no floor over the lava sea. **The front** (`portal_front`): the face with an open standing cell over a full block, the nearer when both have one; the walk in starts only on its side, there is no far walk once a front is known (a bridge can cross to the floorless face), and the tunnel goes to the cell behind the standing cell, (22, 35, -18) at the entry portal, through z -18's netherrack. Lava behind the plane is never sealed.
- **`Task::Enter`** is done in any dimension other than the one it began in (`Enter::from`); a task with none reads as the overworld's (the old way in).
- **The overworld**: stairs up in rock (`stairs_to_surface`) to the open sky, never the caves (overworld-leg wall 1): the climb's stairs are dry (water beside a cut cell refused) and in rock (`Stairs::rock`, the climb's first cell: past `ROCK_FROM` 4 on the plane from it, a cell to cut that is already open is a cave and refused, "cave at", the heading turned; the stairs' end note counts "turned from a cave ahead"); stone pickaxes from the stairs' cobblestone when the stone uses fall under `climb_pick_uses` (`CLIMB_STEPS`: a table placed, then pickaxes to the base's 100 uses), at most `pick_runs`. On the surface: a hut each night (`hut_step`), and in it the night's cook (`home_cook`: with raw meat held and the fuel for all of it, `fuel_ticks_held` counting overworld planks, logs as four planks, coal, and sticks over the reserve with what nether wood makes of them (a stick burns 100 whatever planks made it; a warped stem is 8 sticks); by day too, before eating, from food `COOK_FOOD_FLOOR` 6; by day with the fuel short a walk to a log that burns seen within `FUEL_LOG_REACH` 32, at most `FUEL_WALKS` 6 ("home: a log for the cook's fuel"); the clean flow's cook phase runs through `decide` with `home_cook` set: table and furnace placed, the meat smelted; "home: cook in the hut", "home: the cook over", or "home: no cook, the fuel short"), eat any safe food under `fed` 18, else hunt (`choose_prey`), else look for animals (`animal_walks`); fed, a rest toward `heal_to` for at most `heal_ticks`; then "home: over, fed on the surface" and `home_done()`; the planner then idles in the overworld (never back into the portal) until the eyes' phase takes it.

- **Lava edges** (agent Q8, walls 21 to 25 of `docs/problems/eyes-home.md`): any touch of lava is a death in the Nether (the fire is not put out). Walks and the bridge move sneak at a lava edge, and so does an idle body (`walk::lava_edge`, [[walk-and-watchdog]]). Rails (`rail_wanted`) are laid down to `RAIL_FLOOR` 4 filler, under the keep, on a side that drops to lava. By a lava edge (`near_lava_edge`) a large or medium cube (`cube_size`, metadata 16) is never struck: a split puts two to four cubes by the edge ("home: a large cube not struck by a lava edge"). A span whose west end is reached with no span beyond is walked out (`spans_out`, "home: the old bridges walked out at"). A restock that dug for filler and found no cell leaves it owed (`filler_owed`), run at the next place with cells before the next leg ("home: the restock owed begun at"). A tunnel on the way home never cuts a fortress block (`tunnel::Limits::bricks_wall`; race 45753006452 lane 1 opened a corridor and met wither skeletons). The guard (`guard_wanted`, `home_guard`; Bridger: "do the rails always"): a feet-level block on each open side across the axis of travel, laid before the next step, mob or none, filler down to 0; its filler restocked once at the first span met (2 a span cell, at most `GUARD_EXTRA` 128 over the keep). A fight at a lava edge sneaks; a cube the trip does not fight is never fought though it struck. The restock never digs a floor cell within `SEA_FLOOR` 2 over lava near.
- **The fortress stretch** (agent Q11, `docs/problems/eyes-fortress.md`): the guard never lays a block in a cell the body's box overhangs (`fortress_way::body_in_cell`, in `guard_wanted`; the rail under it still): race 45760537530's three lanes clicked one refused guard 300 times while blazes burned one to 2.4 ("fortress way: no guard in the body's own cell"). No fortress turn in Home while a point steers (`home_way_steers`, also in the tick between a point reached and the next aimed at): the same lanes turned 48 south at their first point ("fortress way: no fortress turn on the way home"). Within `[fortress_way] reach` 250 of the portal and out of `[portal_way] reach` 120, the trip goes by the covered tunnel toward the portal itself, Q6's target, so the last leg runs on unbroken (race 45763915181's heading legs over the fortress and the valley wandered 4,000 ticks): "fortress way: the covered tunnel toward", "fortress way: tunnel toward" every tenth stretch; with no route, the heading's legs ("fortress way: no covered tunnel, the heading's legs"). Once that tunnel has begun, `[fortress_way] junk` is dropped like `[survival] junk` (netherrack kept to 128): race 45766983995's packs filled with what the tunnel cut and every wooden pickaxe craft failed.

- **The End** (issue 20e, [[end-island]]): `rail_wanted` counts a drop to the void as it does lava, and `at_lava_edge` (the idle sneak) a void edge, both only in the End; the End's bridge reuses `guard_next` for its rails.

## Gotchas

- Not built yet: the old stairs as the way up; the tunnel's own lava seal at the ledge (the tunnel's stairs refuse lava beside a cell instead).
