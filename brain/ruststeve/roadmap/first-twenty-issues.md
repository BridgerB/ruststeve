# The first twenty issues

The plan, one issue at a time; each issue's checkpoint start exists before its gameplay code, and it closes only when its done-condition holds on the real race at the stated k/n ([[issues-are-a-plan]]). Amended for ruststeve where Bridger decided otherwise (no Postgres, binary recordings).

## The machine (1–8)
1. **Datagen and protocol tables**: the 26.1.2 reports and jar into committed tables. *Done:* generated by the datagen binary, committed; a captured handshake-to-play stream decodes with zero unknown ids ([[cc-datagen]], [[wire-packet-ids]]).
2. **Connect, log in, stay in-world**: handshake, login, configuration, play, keep-alive, compression, teleport ack; the reducer pure, the socket in its own crate. *Done:* a 10-minute session on a runner's server; a property test shows every keep-alive answered exactly once ([[wire-connection-phases]]).
3. **The world value and chunk sections**: paletted sections as received, `block_at`, entities, inventory, health. *Done:* a captured clojurecraft recording's chunk stream decodes to the same block states at 100 sampled positions ([[wire-chunk-sections]]).
4. **Record and replay, commit-pinned**, plus `why` (fold to time t, print every planner branch's answer). *Done:* a 10-minute run replays with zero mismatches; replay refuses another commit's file without `--force` ([[recording-format]]).
5. **Vanilla physics** as a pure function. *Done:* under 0.01 blocks of error over a recorded 5-minute walk against the server's corrections ([[cc-physics]]).
6. **The server model and its parity table**: blocks, digging, drops, fluids, mobs, simulation distance and tick rate from the race config. *Done:* the same 50 recorded moments in sim and real, outcome classes matching within a stated tolerance ([[cc-sim]], [[measurement-rules]]).
7. **CI**: test, sim race, real race, probe, results data; runner-only compute. *Done:* a push runs the suite; a dispatch runs N real and N sim bots and publishes k/n per stage and commit ([[runners-not-the-mac]]).
8. **Case files and the data contract**: killer, first hit, intent and why for every ending; per-second senses; batch files and the snapshot-stream schema. *Done:* every ended run in a batch has a case (coverage 1.0). (The handoff's version wrote these to Postgres for steve; ruststeve writes files, [[data-out]].)

## The stages (9–20)
| # | Issue | Checkpoint start | Done (real race) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | walk with a pathfinder, node budget, progress invariant | landings | 95% of 200 sim landings reach a log 30+ blocks away; no walk 60 s without progress |
| 10 | wood and the 2×2 grid | spawn | 90% hold a crafting table in 5 game minutes |
| 11 | stone tools, safe stairs down, hardness | table | 90% |
| 12 | survival basics: escape water and lava, eat, defend, never fight under danger health | stone tools | deaths under 5% over 1 game hour |
| 13 | iron kit, fuel as a need | stone | 50% reach the kit in 30 game minutes |
| 14 | water, lava, the obsidian cast as a build plan | kit | 50% light a portal |
| 15 | Nether entry and the fortress; tunnels never beside lava; a building-block reserve | nether | 30% reach a fortress; median bot keeps 32+ blocks |
| 16 | blaze rods: pocket, fight in reach, cover from fireballs read by damage type | fortress | 10% reach 7 rods (clojurecraft's stage 17 passed at 7%) |
| 17 | getting to endermen: a heading that holds, never paces, restocks filler; frozen endermen known | rods | 60% within 32 blocks of a ticking enderman in 15 game minutes. The part clojurecraft never solved |
| 18 | ender pearls: provoke from cover, one at a time, vanilla anger and teleport rules | forest | 5% real and 5% sim reach 12 pearls |
| 19 | eyes of ender and the stronghold: triangulation as a pure function | pearls | 20% stand in the portal room |
| 20 | the End and the dragon | portal room | 1 real win |

A failure inside an issue gets `docs/problems/<stage>.md`, not a new issue. An issue too big splits into 17a, 17b.
