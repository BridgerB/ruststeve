## What was fixed today (each confirmed by the server log, an RCON probe or race.db first)

- The pour is aimed at the cup's north wall face, so it no longer lands in the bot's own cell.
- The SDK has vanilla block hardness and pickaxe-block tables; hand-speed digging of granite and tuff is gone.
- `goto_goal` drives the connection between A* runs; this was the root cause of the stall and `disconnect.timeout` kicks.
- A disconnected bot exits with code 3 instead of running as a ghost.
- Dimension change and login set the world height, fixing nether blocks that read 64 too low.
- Refill safety:
  - never scoop a source at or above the feet
  - never scoop in a column the body touches
  - never dig one's own floor
  - the footing guard climbs a level when nothing is safe
- The roofed-lake move-toward only targets lava below the feet.
- Open roof holes beside the stand are plugged, only above the pool's surface.
- The pad is re-laid after every refill; a refill had dug through it.
- Cups are re-dug until clear of falling gravel.
- After a missed pour, the bot retreats if lava is at its feet.
- The mold never starts as a corpse.
- The descent loop respawns on death; before, it ran on as a ghost for 3 min.

- The drop loop requires solid ground two below for both dig paths.
- The lighting check polls every interior cell for up to 60 ticks.
