## Walls

### 1. The climb by stairs is too slow, and its pickaxe run walks back to the portal (race 45727358847, 4fe8848, 2 lanes x 1 bot, cap 24,000)
- **What happened** (lane 0's ledger; lane 1 is the same world and did the same): from (125, -53, -107) at tick 60, "the climb begun"; by tick 12,000 both bots stood at (126, -19, -84), 34 blocks up, food 11 to 8, 19.8 health, no mob near. Each leg of six stairs took 620 to 840 ticks in deepslate (about 120 ticks a block of height: three digs a stair at about 23 ticks each with a stone pickaxe). At 4,620 "home: pickaxes for the climb (1)": "table skipped (a table near and 9 sticks held)", then "craft stone_pickaxe at the table (124, -53, -106)", the portal room's table 32 below: the bot walked back down its stairs, crafted, and from 5,380 to 6,440 its stairs from the portal room failed ten times (NoPath, floors of air) and once boxed by a cave; it was back at y -29 by 7,180. The second run (10,480) began the same way and placed its own table only after the far craft failed Unopened; that table was left behind. The job's 45 minutes ran out at tick 16,800 with both bots at (117, -21, -83) and (116, -20, -83), "no progress for 840 ticks", food 7, 19.8 health: no damage, no deaths, never near the surface. (Minutes: the server ran under 20 ticks a second; a cap of 24,000 wants 60.)
- **Cause**: a stair costs three digs a block of height; the base's table rule counts any table within `TABLE_SEARCH` (64) as near.
- **Fix** (`arrival.rs`): a one-wide shaft straight up in rock: the cell over the head dug, a jump, a block put into the cell the feet left at the top of the jump (the pearls hunt's proven pillar). One dig a block, sealed below by its own blocks and walled by rock. Refused at a fluid in or beside the cell, a falling block in or over it, a cave beside or over it (unless the sky is over it), or a block too slow to break; then the stairs take a leg and the shaft is tried again where it ended. And the climb's pickaxe run refuses tables past `TABLE_REACH` (5), so the base places the carried one where the bot stands, and digs it back once the run is over. Ledger: "arrival: shaft up from", "arrival: shaft over at … (why; risen N in T ticks)", "arrival: no shaft from … (why)", "arrival: the table at … is far", "arrival: the table at … taken along".
- **Unit tests**: `crates/plan/tests/arrival.rs`.

### 2. Shafts that never rose, drops a full pack could not take, and junk that came back (race 45731721046, c33d1a0, 2 lanes x 1 bot)
- **What happened** (lane 0; lane 1 the same world, the same cells): 10 shafts begun ("arrival: shaft up from"), 12 blocks risen in them by tick 13,500 against 3 "no shaft from" refusals (a cave over the head in the portal room, caves beside). Six of the ten dug the cell over the head and never rose ("no rise for 600 ticks … (1 dug, 0 placed)"); the senses put the body at (124.7, -44.0, -109.5) through the first. The one that ran from a centred body (113.5, -2, -76.5) rose 8 in 631 ticks (about 80 ticks a block against the stairs' 120). Three ended "the dig of … failed Uncollected" with 1 main slot free. The table fix fired: "the table at (121, -21, -111) is far", "(124, -53, -106) is far", the carried table placed at (124, -33, -99), the pickaxe made and "the table at (124, -33, -99) taken along". From 13,800 to the end (16,740) the second pickaxe run stood at (113, 24, -64), food 5, in "base: drop Granite from slot 25 (1 main slots free, 4 kept free)" 59 times: each stack thrown came back at once in the pit.
- **Cause**: a body off the cell's centre touches the next column and its jump is stopped by the rock over it; a dig counted failed when its drop is not taken though its cell is open; the junk of the trip (soul soil, nylium, wart blocks, tuff, granite) fills the pack and cannot be thrown away underground.
- **Fix**: the feet go to the cell's centre (within `CENTRE` 0.12, sneaking) before each jump; a failed dig with its cell open counts as dug; the shaft puts the junk blocks under its feet first (`SHAFT_BLOCKS`: soul soil, nylium, wart blocks, smooth basalt, granite, tuff, diorite, andesite, calcite, dirt, then cobbled deepslate, cobblestone, filler), so the climb empties the pack. The "no rise" note now gives the body's position, whether it is on the ground and the block over the head.
