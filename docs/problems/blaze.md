# Issue 16: blaze rods (one bot first)

## Needs
From the frontier fortress snapshot, one real bot holds 7 blaze rods (`rods_7`), its snapshot published and made the frontier (`brain/ruststeve/decisions/one-bot-first.md`). Races run `revive=false`: a death ends the attempt (Bridger, 2026-11-20: the compute stays in the Nether).

## Done (world terms)

## Risks
Blazes hover out of reach; burning (45% of clojurecraft's lost health; no water in the Nether); volleys in the open (9 of 20 of clojurecraft's deaths); the fortress maze between the gate and the spawner; tick-paced swings bunching on a loaded server; no food to heal with.

## 16a: food first, then a fresh frontier
- **Why**: the frontier fortress snapshot (race 44678197603) came from a bot that started beside a built portal with no food; healing between blaze fights needs food over 18. Bridger (2026-11-20): the bot gathers enough food before the Nether, and a fresh snapshot is taken once it is ready.
- **Built**: `food_reserve` 32 hunger points in the survival rules; `eat::food_points`; the `hunt` row also fires while the `race`/`nether` aim is short of the reserve in the overworld; with a portal to enter and the reserve short, the bot explores for animals before stepping in ("provisions N of 32"); `snap.toml` lists the held items.

## 16b to 16d: the first cut
- **The `spawner` stage** (`world::stage::Stage::Spawner`, `at_spawner`): the feet within `SPAWNER_NEAR` 8 of a spawner seen exposed, between `fortress` and `rods_7`; the race snapshots it like any stage.
- **`spawner_task`** (the `race` aim once the fortress is reached): 7 rods held is the aim met; a rod lying within 8 on a found route is picked up first; at the spawner the bot holds its ground; a spawner seen is walked to on a found route, else tunnelled toward; with none, the nearest brick not within 8 of the trail, else explore.
- **Blazes as shooters**: `blaze` joins the `ranged` list, so `defend` answers one within 16, closes in and strikes; a shooter beyond `defend_radius` is engaged only at `engage_health` 18 or more.

### 1. Under the spawner, no way up (race 44781414716, 44886cb, frontier fortress snapshot, 7 lanes x 1, cap 24,000)
- **What happened**: 0 of 7 reached the spawner; 5 of 7 died (fire 2, `onFire.player blaze`, `fireball blaze`, `arrow piglin`). A unit test holds the step's cells with the race's numbers.
- **Raced** (2443cf3, race 44789031743, frontier fortress snapshot, 7 x 1): **3 of 7 reached the spawner** ("stairs up toward the spawner" 21 times; `spawner` snapshots from lanes 1, 2 and 4). All 7 died: wither skeletons 5 (`mob wither_skeleton` 3, `wither.player wither_skeleton` 2), blazes 2.

### 7. No pickaxe, no table in reach, not enough wood for one (the same race, lanes 1, 3, 11, 12)
- **What happened**: 4 of the 7 ended lanes gave up `stuck` ("12 tasks failed in a row with none done") at (49, 47, -171) or (43, 56, -175), health 20, holding 2 planks, 4 sticks, 114 cobblestone, the water bucket and no pickaxe.
- **Read** (the case files' last 30 notes on lanes 1 and 11): the needs planner's next step is `craft stone_pickaxe at the table (28, 6, -156)`, the table the bot placed 40 blocks below and 25 across; it fails `Unopened` every 700 to 1,300 ticks. Between, "walk to log" to the logs at y 67 to 70 fails NoProgress (a route found, the bot not getting nearer from inside its stair shaft) and explore fails NoProgress. `stairs_to_log` never fires again, because it runs only when a log has no route at all. Two planks are not enough for a new table (four), so the planner can neither reach a table nor place one.
- **Cause**: three things together, the first structural. (1) Nothing keeps a pickaxe: the stone pickaxe (131 uses) and the wooden one (59) wear out on the stairs to the water and the logs, and no step replaces one while the table is near, so a bot far from the table is left with no tool. (2) The table is never carried after the bucket is made: it is left at (28, 6, -156). (3) A walk to a log that fails NoProgress from under a roof is not treated like one with no route, so the climb does not resume.
- **Not fixed: the loop stopped here** (the 12-hour rule: no bot past `gear` since the snapshot of 44840801185, about 23 hours). Candidate fixes, for Bridger to choose: a kept reserve, so that leaving the table with fewer than two pickaxes or without wood for a table crafts them first (structure, per AGENTS.md section 4); taking the table along after the bucket (`carry_table` already exists); `stairs_to_log` also on a failed walk from under a roof; and a fresh `gear` snapshot from a bot on the surface, since every lane of this snapshot starts in the same cave with the same pool and the same far trees.
- **Re-read with full observability** (issue 16e, phase H: `replay ledger` over lane 1's recording of race 44970722653, made before any of 16e, folded with the new decoders; probe 44998831216): the stone pickaxe fell to 65, 32 and 13 uses left at server ticks 6,040, 6,920 and 9,500 and **broke at 9,820 during "stairs down (no exposed IronOre in sight with a route)"**: after the bucket the needs planner went down for more iron with 13 uses left, not up toward the trees, so the cause above is half wrong. The wooden pickaxe fell to 29, 14 and 5 and broke at 12,140 during "stairs up toward the log". Food never fell under 13 (one beef eaten at 40,660): no starvation in this lane. The craft at the table 40 blocks below failed Unopened at plan ticks 41,736, 42,419 and 43,310, then the planner stopped. Read in ten minutes from one file, where the case file had said only "items lost: stone_pickaxe 1, wooden_pickaxe 1".
- **Raced with full observability** (race 45000585321, d5ac31d, the same snapshot, 6 x 1, cap 72,000; the first race whose coverage row is whole but one gap, fixed in 2acc083): all six bots broke the stone pickaxe at ticks 9,640 to 9,920 during "stairs down" (for iron, after the bucket), and the wooden one at 11,980 to 12,240 during "stairs up toward the log". Three then reached the surface at y 56 to 68 beside the trees with no pickaxe and failed every walk to a log NoProgress or NoPath until they ended `no-progress` after 20 minutes; lane 0 made one more stone pickaxe, broke it at 37,080, and held a `defend` against a skeleton for 23,420 ticks at health 1 (a wall of its own, below).
- **Cause** (structural): the planner spends digs with no account of what is left. `goals.toml` gives mine-iron and the other digging rows a spare pickaxe, but (1) a worn pickaxe counts as the spare, and (2) a spare that needs wood is pursued only with a log at the bot's height (`gather_now`), so underground it never is: the bot mines on until every pickaxe is gone and the trees are 40 blocks of stone away.
- **Fix** (clojurecraft's `picks.clj` and `make.clj`): `without_worn`: a tool with fewer than `spare_uses` (40) uses left is no spare. And a dig budget: the uses left on every held pickaxe plus a stone pickaxe's 131 for each one the held wood and stone make (with a table held or near). Under `travel_reserve_digs` (131, clojurecraft's `travel-reserve-digs`), a spare that needs a gather is worth it from anywhere, so the bot turns to the trees (walk, or `stairs_to_log` from under rock) while it can still dig its way there. Unit tests on the needs planner: a worn pickaxe is not a spare; with digs short the spare's gather is chosen underground; with digs to spare it is not.
- **Raced** (82949d2, race 45017249540, the same snapshot, 14 x 1, cap 216,000): coverage whole but for 10 entity-data reads at `PARTICLES`. The fix fired: bots went for wood when low on digs and made new stone pickaxes again and again (lane 4 six times). Four bots (lanes 4, 5, 9, 11) got further than any from this snapshot: iron, flint and steel, a water bucket, and down to lava at y -55 for the portal cast. None passed `gear`; that wall is wall 8.

### 8. The portal cast with no pickaxe (race 45017249540, 82949d2, lanes 4, 5, 9, 11)
- **What happened**: at the cast site deep underground, each bot's last pickaxe broke during the cast or while digging toward the site's landing (lane 4 at tick 58,940 inside a cast; lanes 5, 9, 11 on "dig toward the landing"). None made another, though each held 22 to 26 sticks and 177 to 338 stone (lane 5 a crafting table too). Lane 4's one cast ran 104,600 ticks, placing cobbled deepslate every 400 ticks and digging nothing; lanes 5, 9 and 11 dug toward landings by hand until they ended `no-progress`.
- **Read** (the four case files' tool stories and last two minutes; the code): the cast and the landing digs are chosen only when the needs planner answers `Done` for the `portal` row, whose `holds = [["tag:pickaxes", 1]]`. With no pickaxe held it still answered `Done`.
- **Cause**: `make_with` resolves a goal row's `provides` and `needs` only, never its own `holds` and `spares` (those are resolved only for producer rows, in `resolve_row`). So the portal goal's pickaxe was never asked for; clojurecraft keeps it by its build planner remaking the pickaxe where the bot stands (`build/pickaxe-at-hand?`). And a running cast never looks at its tools, so it went on without one.
- **Fix**: `make_with_wear` resolves the goal's own `holds` and then its `spares` (the same rules as a producer row's) before its provides, so with no pickaxe the portal goal crafts one from held sticks and stone (placing a held table first). A running cast with no pickaxe left pauses: the task ends with its site kept, the planner makes a pickaxe, and the cast resumes at the same site. Unit test: the portal goal with no pickaxe and the makings for one asks for the craft.
