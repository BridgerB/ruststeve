# Issue 16: from the fortress to the blaze spawner

## Needs
A bot that stands at a Nether fortress with the kit a real bot carries in (iron helmet, chestplate and leggings, an iron sword, four stone pickaxes, a table, both buckets, flint and steel, about eleven raw meat) reaches the `spawner` stage: the feet within `SPAWNER_NEAR` 8 of a blaze spawner seen exposed, alive and healthy enough to fight (14 health or more).

## Done (world terms)
One case file shows `stage reached: spawner` with the bot at 14 health or more when the stage is taken, and the ledger lines of each fix below counted.

## Risks
Wither skeletons on the bridges (8 a hit bare, 5.1 through iron, and the wither effect, which armour does not stop); blazes shooting along an open bridge; ghasts; lava under every bridge; raw meat heals little (three hunger points an item, about two health).

## The development start
- **`dev-fortress`** (`config/starts.toml`; development only, never issue-16 evidence, never the frontier): no armoured, fed bot had ever stood at a fortress, and none could until the Nether walk is passed. The start resumes the old frontier snapshot (`race-44678197603/snapshot-fortress-44678197603-race-44678197603-lane-0.tar.zst`: the world, the bot's place at (22, 55, 129) in the tunnel it dug, its memory, 17 health and 17 food) and then swaps the bare kit over RCON (`clear`, then `give`) for what rs-l05-b00 held in the equipped Nether snapshot of race 45194253932, less the overworld's stone. The planner puts the armour on. A place on a fresh world of the same seed was not used: the snapshot bot stands in its own tunnel, which a fresh world does not have.
- **What the first ticks show** (race 45334573113, both lanes read): the kit arrives at tick 20; "eat porkchop" at tick 40 to 60 (hurt at 17 with food 17: the eat-to-heal rule), food 20 at tick 80, three "put on the armour" notes, three "rest" notes, and 18 health at tick 140, when the spawner task starts. A hurt bot with food eats and comes back in 120 ticks; the bare bots of race 45124122633 rested for 24,000.

## Walls

### 1. Backing away from a wither skeleton that walks as fast as the bot (race 45334573113, b58ef19, `dev-fortress`, 3 x 1, cap 12,000)
- **What happened**: lanes 0 and 2 both died, at ticks 4,500 and 4,340, neither at the spawner and neither with one swing. Both cut stairs for 2,500 ticks from (22, 55, 129) to the bridge at (60, 61, 149), then "walk to the spawner at (129, 64, 159)" along the bridge at y 61, z 186 to 189, as far as (110, 61, 186) and (121, 61, 186): 28 blocks from the spawner. There "defend WitherSkeleton 118 (Retreat)" and "defend WitherSkeleton 119 (Retreat)".
- **Read** (both ledgers and senses): lane 0's retreat ran 900 ticks. Senses rows: (105, 61, 187) at tick 3,640, (68, 61, 188) at 3,840, (62, 62, 158) at 4,040, (61, 61, 124) at 4,240, the wither skeleton 7 to 12 blocks behind in every row; then one hit (20 to 14.9) at tick 4,400 and the bot is in lava at (62, 28, 104): `death.attack.lava.player … wither_skeleton`, "9 steps held back". Lane 2's ran 700 ticks back to (66, 58, 153), the skeleton at 6, 4, 2, 1 blocks, then four hits of 5.1 in 40 ticks (20, 14.9, 9.8, 4.6, dead), "0 swings, 0 landed".
- **Cause**: a retreat ends when the foe is `flee_clear` 20 blocks off. A creeper gives up; a wither skeleton chases at the bot's own walking pace (the bot does not sprint), so the gap never opens. The retreat only takes the bot 100 blocks back from its goal with its back turned, until it runs out of route (a bridge end, a drop over lava) and is struck from behind every 20 ticks. `never_fight` for wither skeletons was chosen for bare bots (blaze.md wall 2: 8 a hit, 5 of 7 dead); those same bare bots, fighting, won 4 of 8 fights with a stone sword in 3 to 5 swings and no hit taken. Backing away has now lost 2 of 2 with armour on.
- **Fix**: wither skeletons leave `never_fight` (`config/goals.toml`): an armoured bot with an iron sword fights one that comes within `defend_radius`, as it fights a zombie, and backs away only at `danger_health`. Creepers stay. The note that counts it is the fight's own: "defend WitherSkeleton … (Fight)" and its "defended: n swings, m landed … health a to b".
- **Unit test**: `a_wither_skeleton_within_reach_is_fought` (`crates/plan/tests/foe.rs`, on the real goal table): Fight at full health, Retreat at the danger line; a creeper is still backed away from.
- **Also seen, not this wall**: a skeleton 7 to 10 blocks over the eye was "fought" twice a lane for 280 to 480 ticks with no swing (it never hit; the ignore rule then held). The stairs alternate "stairs up" and "stairs down" six steps at a time toward a spawner 9 blocks higher and 107 away, and wore one pickaxe from 131 uses to 14 in 38 blocks.

### 2. A spawner on record, and an explore the other way (the same race, lane 1)
- **What happened**: lane 1 lived to the cap at 20 health and never came nearer the spawner than 74 blocks. It broke two pickaxes and ended at (42, 64, 76), 120 blocks from the spawner, on "explore toward … (no spawner and no unvisited bricks in view)".
- **Read** (its ledger and senses): the same stairs as the other lanes to (55, 63, 134) at tick 2,420. Then "defend Skeleton 64 (Fight)" for 300 ticks, ended "Retreat … foe up to 8.4 over the eye", the bot back at (46, 60, 133) in its own tunnel. Two "stairs up toward the spawner" from there failed "StepFailed after 0 stairs" at ticks 2,660 and 2,680, the third stall refused the spawner, and at tick 2,700 "explore toward (43, 62, 85)", then "(8, 55, 63)": 360 and 420 ticks of walking away. From tick 3,480 it cut stairs and a tunnel toward the spawner from there, with "explore … failed: NoPath" 28 times between, to the cap.
- **Cause**: `spawner_task` treats a refused spawner as no spawner: it falls through to the corridor search and then to `explore_walk`, whose heading knows nothing of the spawner. One refusal (600 ticks) costs the bot its place.
- **Fix**: with a spawner on record and no walk or dig to it, the bot goes back along its own trail to the mark nearest the spawner (`trail_toward`: a mark at least 4 nearer than the feet, on a found route), and with none nearer waits out the refusal where it stands. It explores only when no spawner is on record, or one is refused for good. Notes: "back toward the spawner at … by the trail mark …" and "wait for the spawner at …".
- **Unit tests**: `the_trail_mark_nearest_the_spawner_is_the_way_back` and `no_mark_nearer_than_the_feet_is_no_way_back` (`crates/plan/tests/spawner.rs`, the lane's own positions).
- **Not read**: why the two stairs failed after 0 stairs with no refusal noted (the stairs are not this file's).

### 3. Resting "out of sight" in the open (race 45335627948, 360791f, agent A4's `dev-spawner` start, 3 x 1: armoured bots at the spawner)
- **What happened**: 3 of 3 died while the planner noted "rest (health … under 18; out of sight)", 87 times in lane 0 alone.
- **Read** (the three ledgers and senses): lane 0 rested on the open bridge at (129, 61, 180 to 188), 20 to 29 blocks south of the spawner, from tick 240. It healed twice (11 to 17.7 by tick 860; 7.8 to 15.4 by tick 1,820) and went back to fight; the third rest took "hit fireball by blaze" or "alight (fireball)" at ticks 1,900, 2,060, 2,240 and 2,440 with blazes at 18 to 20 blocks in the senses rows, and it died there at 0.4 health, eating its beef. Lane 2 the same at (131, 61, 181): "alight (fireball)" at tick 1,460 with a blaze at 16 and one at 20, dead at 1,500. Lane 1 took cover three times, at (131, 62, 173 to 177), each a cell in fire ("escape-fire" five times, "hit in_fire" four) with a piglin's arrows from 5 to 7 blocks (four hits), and died to the piglin at tick 620.
- **Cause**: `cover::COVER_RANGE` was 16: `shooters` returned no blaze beyond it, and `take_cover` answers "rest … out of sight" when no shooter is in range. A blaze shoots from 48. Piglins and skeletons were not shooters at all. A cell in fire counted as cover. And on a bridge there is no cell out of sight to walk to within 6: with none, `take_cover` gave up and the spawner walk went on.
- **Fix** (`plan::cover`, `Plan::take_cover`):
  1. Blazes and ghasts count from 48 blocks, piglins and skeletons from 16 (`COVER_RANGE`, `NEAR_RANGE`).
  2. The rest is judged where the body is (`seen_body`: lines to its eye and middle from where it stands, not from the middle of its cell), every look (20 ticks), and a body touching fire is never at rest.
  3. Seen or burning: the nearest cell out of sight within 8 on a found route, never the cell it stands in ("take cover at …").
  4. None: the bot builds its cover where it stands. `shelter_block` takes the first line of sight that reaches the body, finds the cell it comes in through, and puts a held block there (cobblestone first; netherrack last, since a fireball lights it for good), clicking the floor under it or a block beside it; a cell with nothing to click gets what it needs under it first (a roof over the head hangs on a wall three high). One block every 10 ticks until no shooter sees the body: two blocks for one blaze level with the bot, ten for a closed box. Note: "shelter block n at … (… on … face …)". Every cell read is one a line of sight passes or one beside the body.
  5. Healed, the bot leaves through its own wall: the spawner walk finds no route out of a closed box, and `dig_toward` cuts one.
- **Unit tests** (`crates/bot/tests/cover.rs`): `a_blaze_twenty_blocks_off_is_a_shooter`, `a_shelter_is_built_where_the_line_of_sight_comes_in`, `a_roof_hangs_on_a_wall_three_high`.
- **Not done**: digging a hole instead (a bridge deck is one block over air or lava); the retreat of a fight still runs where its route goes (agent A4's). For A4: `cover::cover_cells(w, &cover::shooters(w))` is the public list of cells out of sight, nearest first.
