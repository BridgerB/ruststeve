# Briefing for the strategy agent — end of cycle 2 (2026-10-02)

## State in a paragraph

Cycle 2 made the measurements trustworthy, took the safety failures apart one by one, and **met gate 1 (surface survival)**: the Phase D natural race ran 5 bots for 4 hours with **zero deaths**, through thousands of breath-watchdog pre-emptions. It met none of its own phase gates. The Phase B surface race failed on one drowning in a roofed aquifer; that case was fixed in the water gym (water_roofed 10/10, zero deaths in 50+ water trials) before the Phase D race confirmed survival. The portal gym (Phase C) never reached ≥ 8/10 at < 15 min with zero deaths; its best batch was 6/9 clean (median 19.7 min). Across ten batches the failure mix moved from deaths (death loops, void falls, lava-ringed drops, a server crash) to time: by batch 9 every trial timed out with 2 deaths in total, and two were within minutes of lighting. At hour 15 the plan's "reduce ambition" rule was applied (cluster ≥ 100 sources, 256 cobble) for batch 10. Batch 10 reached 6 lit portals and 3 clean (fastest 13.5 min). The race reached the nether 0/5: no bot got to the portal stage. The furthest made flint and steel at 54 min, then looped 3 h on a crafting table; the others hit a surface-bobbing water-escape loop or a treeless start. The tree is uncommitted (cycles 1 and 2, 22 files, +2,564 / −2,294); commits were not authorised.

## Decisions for the strategy agent

1. **Gym cadence.** This cycle restarted the portal gym about ten times to retest fixes, so no build was measured twice. Propose: a fixed build is measured by two full batches before the next change, except for a safety bug. Accept?
2. **The portal gate's time target.** With deaths largely solved, the 15-minute median is now the binding constraint. Pursue speed (scoop and refill travel), or accept a looser intermediate target (e.g. < 25 min) to unblock the race gate?
3. **Is gate 1 met?** Race i5 had 0 deaths over 4 h × 5 bots on a clean fresh region, with heavy water exposure (thousands of breath pre-emptions, all survived). But the bots made little progress, so exposure was mostly surface and shallow water, not the deep descent. Count it as met, with a regression race each cycle, or require a race in which bots reach the portal stage?
4. **Commits.** The tree holds two cycles of uncommitted work. Authorise local commits at phase boundaries (never push)?
