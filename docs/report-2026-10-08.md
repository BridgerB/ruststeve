## 11. For the steve agent

These are bugs or designs steve likely shares, since this SDK is a port of typecraft.

1. **A 2×2 craft into a stale container window.**
   - After a death or a walk-away with a table open, our client kept the table as `active_window`. The 2×2 clicks landed in its 3×3 slots, the grid stayed wrong, and `craft` returned Ok with nothing made.
   - Measured 0/6 without the fix vs 6/6 with it.
   - steve's own wood-lock diagnosis (a craft-grid undercount plus a `bot.craft` desync) has the same signature. Check whether typecraft clears its window on respawn and on a server close.
2. **The out-of-liquid ledge lift** passed a bare velocity offset to `does_not_collide` where prismarine-physics passes `pos.offset(...)`, so a swimming bot never climbed a 1-high bank. If typecraft has the same call, it explains steve's "only wall impulse lifts" water behaviour (not checked: typecraft is not on this machine).
3. **Lily pads read as land.** Any "nearest land" search that accepts a block floating on water steers the escape to a pad 1–4 blocks out, against a shore 28–50 blocks out.
4. **Slicing a pathfinder against a wall-clock budget** cut our search to about 45%. The one-shot search took the portal from 7/18 to 11/17.

**Findings carried from steve, and what happened to each:**
- **Radius-count frame and site decisions:** confirmed. Our "already cast" check counted obsidian within 8; it now uses the stored cells (dcd279c), and the 102-attempt hang was reproduced once.
- **"Frame present" fooled by nearby obsidian:** confirmed (the same fix).
- **The 102-dispatch pickaxe loop:** the 4×-same-failure rule is in. `NO PICKAXE` appears 233 times in the cycle-5 logs, a rate-limited line, and the mid-task craft fired once.
- **Respawn ignoring the spawnpoint:** confirmed, with two world-spawn respawns in i8.
- **Avoidance rules that are never evaluated:** confirmed in kind. settle_xz's raw walk had no lava check; it now refuses (197 refusals).
